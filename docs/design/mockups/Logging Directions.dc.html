workout — design exploration 01

Logging screen — three brutalist-paper directions

All three commit to the agreed system: light cream paper, ink-black type, clinical copy, lowercase workout brand, compound coordinates (W2·D1), and an ambient RIR week header. They differ in attitude — how loud the ink is, and what carries the hierarchy.

A — ledger. Quietest. Hairline-ruled accounting table, everything earns its ink. By-the-book brutalism.
B — stamp. Loudest. Black blocks, condensed display numerals, logged sets get a literal red rubber stamp.
C — index. Graph-paper field with offset-shadow index cards; the meso track lives at the top of every screen.
