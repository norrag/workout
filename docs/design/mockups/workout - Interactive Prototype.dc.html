The app shell, made real

A working build of the five-tab shell — Workout · Cycles · Templates · Exercises · More. Tap to navigate. The logging flow runs end to end: step weight & reps, log sets, watch the progress bar fill, finish into the session-feedback sheet, then advance to the next workout. Everything you do persists across reloads. The same component is shown in both directions the design has explored — the paper ledger on the left, the dark-mode system on the right — so they can be judged against each other.
