workout — design exploration 02 · full app screens

Ledger system, built out

Locked from your feedback: direction A's ruled-table discipline + C's meso track header on every workout screen. Mono typeface dropped — everything is Archivo, with tabular numerals for data. Monochrome ink on paper; orange is budgeted strictly for "current position" and "selected" states. Weights/reps sit in boxed fields to read as editable; every exercise gets history + an overflow menu; every set gets a handle menu.
